The Details

Each Step Explained

01
📤
Submit Your Design

Share your artwork, photos, or creative brief through our quote form, email, or by calling us directly. We work with what you have — from polished vector files to rough sketches.

  • Accepted: PNG, JPG, PDF, AI, EPS, PSD, SVG
  • 300 DPI+ for photos & images
  • Vector files preferred for logos
  • No artwork? Our designers can help
02
🖥️
Proof & Approval

Within 48 hours of receiving your files, we'll prepare a full digital proof showing exactly how your design will print — including colors, sizing, and placement. You review, request adjustments, and approve.

  • Digital proof delivered in 48 hours
  • Unlimited revision rounds until you're happy
  • Color-accurate proof to match final print
  • No production starts without your approval
03
🖨️
Print & Production

Once you approve the proof, your order enters our production queue. We use premium inks and precision equipment, with quality checks at every stage of the printing and finishing process.

  • Production begins immediately after approval
  • Premium water-based & sublimation inks
  • Individual quality inspection on each piece
  • Color consistency guaranteed across all units
04
📦
Ship & Deliver

Your completed order is carefully packaged and shipped via UPS or USPS with full tracking. Typical delivery from proof approval is 7–14 business days, with rush options available.

  • Shipped via UPS or USPS with tracking
  • Free shipping on orders over $150
  • Rush options: 3–5 business days
  • Gift packaging available on request
File Requirements

Getting Your Files Ready

Accepted Formats

  • PNG — Preferred for photos with transparency
  • JPG / JPEG — Standard photo format
  • PDF — Print-ready layouts and documents
  • AI (Adobe Illustrator) — Best for logos & vector art
  • EPS — Vector format, widely compatible
  • PSD (Photoshop) — Layered files welcome
  • SVG — Scalable vector for web-sourced artwork

Quality Guidelines

  • Minimum 300 DPI at final print size
  • Vector files (AI/EPS/SVG) preferred for logos
  • CMYK color mode when possible
  • Transparent background for logos (PNG or AI)
  • No watermarks on final submitted files
  • Embed all fonts before saving PDF
  • Maximum file size: 25MB per upload
